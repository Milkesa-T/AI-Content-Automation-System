What is RAG in this Project?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) enriches the AI's prompt with custom business guidelines (e.g. Addis Ababa local neighborhoods, hashtag limits, legal disclaimers) stored in the knowledge base, ensuring the output never sounds like generic copy.

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance

No draft is ever automatically published. Every record is stored with status: 'Draft - Review Required', allowing marketing directors to inspect, edit, and click Approve or Reject.
